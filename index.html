03 / PROJECT SPOTLIGHT

PROJECT October 2026/ Data Analysis

One project.
Meaningful insights.

Interactive STOXX Europe 600 dashboard with country and sector filters.

THE CHALLENGE

Financial markets generate enormous amounts of data, yet understanding how investments are distributed across countries, sectors, and companies is not always straightforward for individual investors. This project started with a simple question:
How can publicly available financial data be transformed into an accessible, interactive tool that helps people better understand the European stock market?
Using the iShares STOXX Europe 600 ETF as a starting point, the objective was to make complex portfolio data easier to explore, compare, and interpret through interactive visualisations, without requiring specialised financial knowledge or expensive analytical software.

THE APPROACH

Snapshot data: iShares STOXX Europe 600 ETF (26 October 2026).

THE OUTCOME

The analysis revealed notable differences in the distribution of investments across European countries and sectors.
The United Kingdom emerged as the largest country exposure in the STOXX Europe 600 ETF, representing approximately 23.4% of the portfolio, with financial services accounting for its largest sector allocation. One interesting observation was the relatively limited prominence of the information technology sector across European countries, with the Netherlands standing out as a notable exception, where technology represented the leading sector by ETF portfolio weight.
Overall, the project demonstrated how interactive data visualisation can uncover geographical and sectoral patterns in European equity investments, making complex financial information more accessible and easier to interpret.
